## 2. What data we collect

All data is collected through your interaction with the Bot inside Telegram.
We do **not** ask for, store, or process: your phone number, email address,
real name, payment information, precise geolocation, photos, contacts list,
or any messages you send in other Telegram chats.

### 2.1 Identifiers received from Telegram

When you send a message or tap a button, Telegram passes the following to the
Bot. We persist only the fields marked **[stored]**:

- **Telegram user ID** *(numeric)* — **[stored]** — used as your primary
  account key. Cannot be changed by you.
- **Telegram @username** *(if you have a public handle)* — **[stored]** —
  cached so other users can add you as a friend by @handle. Updated on every
  interaction; if you remove your @username, the next interaction clears it.
- First name, last name, language code, message ID, chat ID — *not stored*.

### 2.2 Data you provide explicitly

- **Timezone** (e.g. `Europe/Moscow`) — chosen from a preset list in Settings.
- **Interface language** (`ru` or `en`).
- **Reminder times** (HH:MM for morning and evening reminders, plus on/off
  toggles).
- **Custom flashcards** — term and definition pairs you create. Up to 100 per
  subject.
- **Custom tasks** — problem text, accepted answers, and an optional hint,
  imported from a `.txt` file you upload.
- **Pet name and customization** — name string, colour, accessory you choose
  for your in-bot digital pet.
- **Free-text messages to administrators** — when you write a message
  intended for the bot operator (not a command), it is logged and forwarded
  to admins.

### 2.3 Data generated by your use of the Bot

- **Study sessions** — start time, duration (1–120 minutes), coins earned,
  optional 1–4 emoji rating.
- **Quiz / MCQ / task / flashcard progress** — for each item: hash of the
  content, number of attempts, success count, last-review timestamp, SM-2
  spaced-repetition parameters (ease factor, interval).
- **Achievements** — progress and completion status against a fixed catalog.
- **Streak** — current consecutive-day count and whether you studied today.
- **In-bot coins** — virtual currency balance (no real-world monetary value).
- **Pet level and XP**, **inventory of purchased items**, last "excited"
  timestamp.
- **Leaderboard scores** — weekly per-user totals (time / task / quiz / card
  points), used for the weekly leaderboard rollover.
- **Streak-freeze records** — when you bought a freeze and what day it
  consumed.
- **Friend relationships** — pending friend requests (from/to user IDs) and
  confirmed friendships (pair of user IDs). Visible to the two users
  involved.
- **Friend invite tokens** — short-lived (3-day) tokens used in
  `t.me/<bot>?start=friend_<token>` deep links.
- **Tip view history** — which productivity tip you've already seen
  (per-tip), the daily tip-of-the-day selected for you, total tip views and
  whether today's coin reward was granted.
- **Event log** — an append-only table of analytics events (e.g.
  `session_completed`, `settings_changed`, `tip_viewed`, `reminder_sent`).
  Each entry contains your Telegram user ID, the event name, a JSON
  properties object, and a timestamp. Used only by the bot operator for
  aggregate product analytics. **No event ever contains free-text you
  entered.**

### 2.4 Operational logs

- **Application log** (`bot.log`) — rotated text log of operational events
  (errors, scheduler ticks, command invocations, broadcast results). May
  contain your Telegram user ID and the names of commands you ran. Retained
  for as long as rotation keeps them (~25 MB total ≈ days–weeks of activity).
- **Database backups** — daily SQLite snapshots taken automatically.
  Retained for **30 days** by default; older backups are deleted
  automatically.

## 5. Who we share data with

We share data with **no third parties** for commercial, marketing, or
profiling purposes. Specifically:

- We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your data.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics services (Google Analytics,
  Amplitude, Mixpanel, etc.). All analytics are computed locally from the
  Bot's own database.
- We do **not** integrate ad networks.

There are three unavoidable parties involved in operating the Bot:

1. **Telegram Messenger Inc.** — the messaging transport. By using the Bot
   inside Telegram, you are also subject to Telegram's own Privacy Policy
   (<https://telegram.org/privacy>). We have no control over what Telegram
   itself stores or where.
2. **The hosting provider** of the server the Bot runs on. The provider has
   access to the encrypted disk where the SQLite database lives but does not
   have application-level access to your data.
3. **Other Bot users** *(only for the friends feature)* — when you accept a
   friend request, the other user can see your Telegram @username, your
   weekly leaderboard score, and your study streak.

## 10. Security

- The database is a local SQLite file on the operator's server, accessible
  only via the operating system's file permissions.
- Backups are stored on the same server.
- Bot administrative commands are gated by Telegram user ID; only the
  operator and explicitly-added admins can run them.
- Rate limiting (30 actions / 60 seconds per user) is applied to prevent
  abuse.
- We do **not** currently encrypt the database at rest. If this is a concern
  for your use case, do not store sensitive content in custom flashcards /
  tasks / messages to administrators.

In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a high risk to
your rights, we will notify affected users without undue delay (GDPR
Art. 34) and the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours (GDPR
Art. 33). In Russia, we will notify Roskomnadzor within 24 hours of becoming
aware of a confirmed leak (152-ФЗ Art. 21 п. 3.1).
